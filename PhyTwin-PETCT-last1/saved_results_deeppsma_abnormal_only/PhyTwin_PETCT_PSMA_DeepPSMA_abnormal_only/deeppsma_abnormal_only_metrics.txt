[What these metrics mean]
  SliceRecall: among abnormal slices, the fraction whose image score exceeds a fixed threshold.
  PatientRecall: among abnormal patients, the fraction with at least one detected abnormal slice.
  Pixel Dice/IoU: spatial overlap between the predicted anomaly mask and the ground-truth lesion mask.
  PixelRecall: how much of the lesion mask is covered by the prediction; high value means fewer missed lesion pixels.
  PixelPrecision: how much of the predicted mask falls inside the lesion; high value means less over-segmentation.
  LesionRecall: fraction of connected lesion regions touched by the predicted mask.
  Score distribution: whether abnormal slices/patients receive high anomaly scores relative to the model calibration.
